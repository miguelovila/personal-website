Markdown is not exciting. That is most of its appeal.

## Plain files age well

The best content systems let the writing leave. A post should not need a production database and three SaaS products just to remain readable in five years.

## Frontmatter is enough until it is not

```md
---
title: "A readable title"
publishedDate: 2026-08-08
tags: [markdown, publishing]
---
```

This is a small contract. It tells the site how to place the writing, and it lets the writing stay human.

## The MDX exception

MDX is useful when the article needs an interactive island, not when every paragraph wants to cosplay as an application.

> The trick is not avoiding power. The trick is making the default path calm.
